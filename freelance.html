A short note

A multi-disciplinary designer with a soft spot for brand work.

I'm a freelance designer based in Chennai, working with clients across the UAE and India. Trained in UI & UX, sharpened in advertising, and grounded in graphic design — my craft sits at the intersection of all three.

Most of my freelance work falls into four buckets: generative AI design (brand-aligned imagery created from detailed, controlled prompts), brand guidelines (turning client resources into a usable system), presentation design (decks that close the room), and the social, web and OOH work that flows out of them.

What I do

Generative AI design

Designs created by AI — brand-aligned imagery generated through detailed, controlled prompts. Used at scale for social, web, ads and in-store collateral.

Brand guidelines

Turn a client's existing resources into a usable system. Type, grid, voice, motion, do's and don'ts — ready for any future designer to pick up.

Presentation design

Decks, sales kits and pitch documents built from the client's content. Editorial layout, clean typography, paced storytelling.
